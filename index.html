Asset Standardizer

Turns messy imported model packs into a clean, consistent project. Point it at a folder of FBX files and textures, review the plan, and apply it in one go.

  • Organizes models, textures, materials and prefabs into a folder layout you define
  • Detects PBR texture maps by their suffixes and applies the right import settings
  • Builds materials for URP Lit or your own custom shaders
  • Shows every change before anything is moved or created
